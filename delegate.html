✦ Delegate a grind, keep the key

The reason nobody sane buys a vanity wallet is that the seller sees the key they sell you. Split-key grinding removes the objection with arithmetic instead of a promise: your browser picks a secret scalar a1 and publishes only the point P1 = a1·B. The remote grinder searches offsets a2 until P1 + a2·B matches your pattern, then hands back a2. It never sees a1, so it cannot compute the final key, and you combine aFinal = (a1 + a2) mod L here, in this tab.

What you get, and the one honest catch

The combined scalar controls the address, signs for it, and is a perfectly valid Solana account. But it is a raw Ed25519 scalar, not a 32-byte seed. You cannot invert SHA-512 to find a seed that expands to a chosen scalar. So a split-key wallet is an expanded key.

That trade is stated here rather than discovered later. If you need a seed-importable key, grind it yourself on the grinder page; if you need a hard pattern and control your own signing, this is strictly better than trusting a seller.
