✦ Solana vanity wallet generator

Grind a Solana wallet address that starts with, ends with, or is bookended by characters of your choice, for your agent, treasury, or anywhere a memorable address matters. Runs entirely in your browser. The secret key is generated on your device and never crosses the network.

Every estimate on this page uses the exact Base58 distribution, not the 58ⁿ approximation every other grinder ships: a leading K is 17× rarer than a leading 5, and pretending otherwise misprices a grind by more than an order of magnitude. Need a pattern too slow for one machine? Delegate it without handing anyone your key.
