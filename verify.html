✦ Verify a certificate

Paste a proof-of-grind certificate. Verification runs in this tab and trusts nothing inside the document: every claim is recomputed from first principles, and the signature is checked against the key ring published at /.well-known/solana-vanity.json rather than the key the certificate names for itself.

What a certificate can and cannot prove

It proves the issuer signed a specific set of facts about a specific address: the pattern, the difficulty under a named model, the rarity, and a freshness nonce that makes re-selling the same address as "freshly ground" detectable. For a split-key grind it additionally proves P1 + a2·B == address, which is a mathematical statement that the issuer could not have held the private key.

It cannot prove that a key was never copied by whoever ran the grinder, unless that grinder was split-key or ran on your own machine. That is why the browser grinder is the default here and the custodial server endpoint is off by default.
